Tiny Planet

A free feelings chart for little ones

Little kids can’t always name a feeling, but they can point to one. This one-page chart has eight feeling friends, from Bunbun (happy) to Wiggle (worried), so your child can show you how today felt, no reading needed.

Download the free printable

A4 PDF · prints fine on US Letter too · free for home and classroom use

How to use it

1Stick it somewhere easy to see, like the fridge or by the bed.
2Ask “Which friend visited you today?” and let them point. Then ask what made that friend visit.
3Just listen. There are no wrong friends. Every feeling is welcome on the planet.

The app version, with Pip

In Tiny Planet, your child taps the friend that visited, then what it was about, and you can look back over the week together. Free on the App Store for iPhone.
