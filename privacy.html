Tiny Planet

A little planet for big feelings.

Privacy

Last updated 18 November 2026.

Tiny Planet collects no data. I have no server, no account system, no analytics and no ads, and the app contains no third-party tracking code. Nothing your family enters is sent to me.

What stays on your phone

Children's names or nicknames, daily feelings, what the day was about, written notes, voice recordings, stickers and settings are stored only on this device. Deleting the app, or choosing "Start over" in Settings, removes them. If you use iCloud Backup, Apple may include app data in your own device backup.

Children

Tiny Planet is set up by a grown-up and made for families. It does not collect personal information from children or from anyone else. The grown-up pages can be locked with a PIN or Face ID.

Permissions the app asks for

Microphone: only while your child holds the star to record a voice note. Recordings never leave the device.
Notifications: the bedtime reminder and pattern notes, scheduled on the phone itself.
Face ID: to keep the grown-up pages private. Apple handles the check; I never see face data.
Photos: only to save a postcard you choose to keep.

Things you choose to share

"Save a copy" creates a backup file that you store wherever you like. Postcards and pictures are shared only when you tap share, through Apple's share sheet.

Purchases

Tiny Planet Family subscriptions are handled by Apple. I never see your payment details.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
