A little planet for big feelings.

Tiny Planet is a feelings diary for young children and the grown-ups who love them. Each day your child tells Pip how it felt, and you get a gentle window into their week.

Hi! I'm Pip.How did today feel?

Tap a friend to tell Pip how today felt.

I built Tiny Planet because "How was your day?" so often gets "fine". A child might not have the word yet, but they can always point to a friend.

A day on Tiny Planet

A check-in is quick, and it's built so a young child can do it with a grown-up nearby.

After school

Tell Pip about today

Your child picks one of eight little friends: Happy, Excited, Okay, Sleepy, Meh, Sad, Worried or Cross. No reading needed.

A moment later

Spin the wheel to say what it was about

School, friends, family, food, screens and more. Pip asks "What made it partly cloudy?", and your child can hold the star to record a short voice note.

Bedtime

Slide the planet to sleep

The sky dims, Pip puts on his nightcap, and your child blows him a goodnight kiss. A small ritual that ends the day softly.

Once they're asleep

See the week in your corner

Behind a grown-up PIN or Face ID, you see how the week felt at a glance, and Pip suggests one gentle question to ask tomorrow.

Small check-ins add up to a picture you can talk about.

Over the weeks, Pip notices patterns, like which days tend to feel heavy, and turns them into one question you can ask at dinner. It's a conversation starter, never a score.

At the end of each month, the friends who visited become a postcard you can keep or send to the grandparents.

Help

The bedtime reminder never arrives

Reminders are local notifications. Check Settings → Notifications → Tiny Planet, and that a Focus mode isn't silencing them. The reminder time is in Parents → Settings.

Pip can't hear my child

Voice notes need the microphone. Turn it on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → Tiny Planet.

I forgot the grown-up PIN

Tap "Forgot PIN?" on the PIN screen, then confirm with your phone's passcode or Face ID to set a new one.

Moving to a new phone

In Parents → Settings, "Save a copy" makes a backup file you keep. On the new phone, "Bring a copy back" restores it.

Cancelling or restoring Tiny Planet Family

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. To cancel or change one, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions. If a purchase doesn't show up, use "Restore purchases" in Parents → Settings.

Is my child's data private?

Yes. There is no account and no server. Everything stays on your iPhone. Read the full privacy policy.
