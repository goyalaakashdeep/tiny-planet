Why I made Tiny Planet

I built it because "How was your day?" so often gets "fine". A child might not have the word yet, but they can always point to a friend.

Each day your child tells Pip how it felt by picking one of eight little friends, then spins a wheel to say what it was about. They can hold the star to record a short voice note. At bedtime they slide the planet to sleep and blow Pip a goodnight kiss. A sticker book and a calm breathing corner round it out.

Behind the grown-up lock, you see the week at a glance, Pip suggests one question to ask, and each month becomes a postcard you can keep or send to the grandparents.

Help

The bedtime reminder never arrives

Reminders are local notifications. Check Settings → Notifications → Tiny Planet, and that a Focus mode isn't silencing them. The reminder time is in Parents → Settings.

Pip can't hear my child

Voice notes need the microphone. Turn it on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → Tiny Planet.

I forgot the grown-up PIN

Tap "Forgot PIN?" on the PIN screen, then confirm with your phone's passcode or Face ID to set a new one.

Moving to a new phone

In Parents → Settings, "Save a copy" makes a backup file you keep. On the new phone, "Bring a copy back" restores it.

Cancelling or restoring Tiny Planet Family

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. To cancel or change one, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions. If a purchase doesn't show up, use "Restore purchases" in Parents → Settings.
