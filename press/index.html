About the app

Tiny Planet is a feelings diary for young children and the grown-ups who love them. I built it because "How was your day?" so often gets "fine", and I wanted a gentle, playful way for a child to show how a day really felt.

Each day, a child tells Pip, a small creature on their own tiny planet, how they feel by picking one of eight little friends, then spins a wheel to say what it was about. They can hold the star to record a short voice note. At bedtime they slide the planet to sleep and blow Pip a goodnight kiss. A sticker book and a calm breathing corner round it out.

Behind a grown-up PIN, parents see the week at a glance. Pip notices patterns over time and suggests one question to ask, and a monthly postcard turns the month into a keepsake.

There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking: everything stays on the family's iPhone. I'm an independent developer, and I designed and built Tiny Planet on my own.
